Pricing questions

How much does it cost?
It depends on the number of branches, your daily volume and the modules you need. We send a written quote with no hidden charges.
How long does it take to go live?
Most labs are live within 3 to 5 working days. We configure your tests and rates, migrate your data and train your staff.
Can you move data from our current software?
Yes. Migration of patients, tests and rate lists is part of onboarding at no extra charge.
What support do you offer?
Phone, email and WhatsApp support 7 days a week, 9 AM to 6 PM, plus refresher training whenever your team needs it.
