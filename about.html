We build software for the people who run labs

Qrynto Tech is a technology company focused on one thing: making laboratory work simpler, faster and easier to trust.

Why we exist

Too many labs still run on registers, spreadsheets and phone calls. Samples go missing, results get retyped, and owners only learn how the day went after it's over.

For more than two years we've been building lab software that is simple enough for a busy front desk and thorough enough for an NABL assessor. Labs across Maharashtra use it every day to register patients, track samples, validate results and deliver reports.

What we believe

Built from the bench

Every feature starts with watching how a lab actually works, from the collection chair to the pathologist's desk.

Honest pricing

A written quote based on what you use. No setup surprises, no charge for updates.

Support that answers

Phone, email and WhatsApp every day of the week, from people who know your setup.

Paper-free by default

Digital reports and records save paper, energy and water, and they are easier to find.
