Where turnaround time really goes, and how barcodes win it back

Operations10 Jan 2026By the Qrynto Tech team

Patients and doctors judge a lab by turnaround time. Yet very little of the delay happens on the analyser. It happens between steps: a tube waiting to be logged, a sample that can't be matched to a patient, a batch nobody noticed had arrived.

Label at the source

Printing a unique barcode at collection means the sample is identified before it leaves the patient. There's no handwriting to decipher and no second registration at the bench.

Scan at every hand-off

Each scan updates the sample's stage. Staff can see what's waiting, and managers can see where samples are piling up while there's still time to act.

  • Fewer mislabelled or mismatched samples
  • No time spent hunting for a tube
  • Delays visible as they happen
  • Turnaround measured accurately for every test
One of our customers cut turnaround time by nearly 40% after switching.

Finish with delivery

Tracking only matters if the report reaches the patient quickly. When approval triggers automatic WhatsApp or SMS delivery, the last delay in the chain disappears.
