Questions labs ask us

More answers on the FAQ page, or ask us directly on WhatsApp.

Which labs is Qrynto Tech for?
Pathology and diagnostic labs, hospital labs, collection centres and multi-branch lab chains. It works for a single location and scales as you add branches.
How long does it take to go live?
Most labs are live within 3 to 5 working days. We configure your tests and rates, migrate your data and train your staff.
Does it connect to our analysers?
Yes. Results come in directly from supported machines and are checked against reference ranges automatically.
Is it ready for NABL accreditation?
Yes. It keeps a full audit trail, supports validation and approval workflows, and includes documentation templates for NABL requirements.
How much does it cost?
It depends on the number of branches, your daily volume and the modules you need. We send a written quote with no hidden charges.
